Frequently asked questions

Will my image be uploaded?

No. Conversion happens locally in your browser. We don't receive your image.

Can JPG have a transparent background?

No. JPG does not support transparency. Choose a background color when converting PNG to JPG.

Will image quality change?

PNG is lossless. JPG uses lossy compression, so PNG to JPG may reduce quality slightly.
